# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static site: a single `index.html` with Tailwind CSS (CDN), vanilla JavaScript, Google Fonts and an icon CDN (pinned by the user's brief). Deployed on GitHub Pages from this repository.

## Users

People in La Paz, Baja California Sur, planning a private or corporate celebration (weddings, XV años, birthdays, company events). They usually arrive from WhatsApp, Facebook or a shared link, mostly on a phone. They come to judge whether the food and presentation are good enough for their event, and then ask for a quote.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for **Eventos ALE — Alexis Carmona Catering**, an event catering and organization business. It shows the services and work, then turns interest into a WhatsApp conversation with a pre-filled quote request. Success means a visitor sends a WhatsApp quote request with enough detail (event type, date, guests, services) for the business to answer.

## Positioning

A single team that brings the banquet, a mobile cocktail bar with a neon-lit counter, themed and dessert tables, and full coordination to the same event in La Paz. The owner's own presence (Alexis Carmona) and the lit bar are what neighbors can't copy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: banquets and catering (custom menus, buffets, plated dishes); mobile cocktail bar (neon-lit bar, craft mixology with and without alcohol, glassware, bartenders); themed and dessert tables (dessert tables, snacks, centerpieces, candy bags); full event organization (logistics, furniture, service staff).
- Events served: weddings, XV años, birthdays and gatherings, corporate events.
- No prices, packages or online booking. Every quote goes through WhatsApp.

- Visual direction pinned by the user's brief: dark charcoal ground (#0F1115 / #161920), champagne gold (#D4AF37), a cyan neon accent (#2DD4BF) for the cocktail bar, off-white and slate text, Playfair Display and Plus Jakarta Sans.

## Product Principles

1. Every path ends in a WhatsApp quote. Nothing should make that harder.
2. Show the food, drinks and setups. People choose by what they see.
3. Be honest about availability: appointments Monday to Thursday, weekends are for events.
4. Phone first. Most visitors arrive from WhatsApp or Facebook on mobile.
