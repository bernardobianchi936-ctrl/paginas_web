# Surface: index.html (landing)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: people in La Paz planning a wedding, XV años, birthday or corporate event, mostly on phones from WhatsApp/Facebook. Action: send a pre-filled WhatsApp quote. Proof: photos of food, drinks and setups (Unsplash stand-ins until the client's own photos arrive), "desde 2016". No testimonials, counts or prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one evening of service: candle-gold banquet light that switches to the cyan neon of their own bar arch when the drinks arrive, and closes at the quote. It refuses the category default of a cream wedding-planner page with a centered headline over identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian #0F1115 and slate #161920 grounds; champagne gold #D4AF37 as hairline rules, the primary button and numerals; cyan #2DD4BF only inside the bar passage, drawn as a neon tube arch (SVG stroke) around a cocktail photo. Playfair Display for headings, Plus Jakarta Sans for text and controls. Photos are full-bleed or tall framed plates, never thumbnails in boxes. Lucide icons, thin stroke.

STORY: Visitor sees a lit banquet and the promise, understands the four services, sees the bar is theirs (neon), recognizes their event type, browses the work, fills five fields, and lands in WhatsApp with a well-written message. Knows Mon–Thu is by appointment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night banquet photo with a dark gradient from the bottom-left; glass nav pill floating at top with wordmark left and "Cotizar por WhatsApp" right; H1 at large display scale bottom-left over the photo, subtitle under it, gold "Cotizar mi evento" plus ghost "Ver servicios"; a meta row under the actions with location pin "La Paz, Baja California Sur" and "Desde 2016". On mobile the H1 still sits in the first screen with the primary button visible.

FORM: Brief-pinned single-page landing (sections and order fixed by the user). Signature interaction: choosing an event type (in the Eventos section) preselects it in the quote form and scrolls there; the form builds the WhatsApp message live in a preview bubble. Motion: one orchestrated reveal per section on scroll (rise + fade from visible-by-default), neon arch flicker-on once when the bar enters. seed: none (pinned) — "Fondo principal: Negro carbón / Obsidiana (#0F1115)… Acentos principales: Dorado champán (#D4AF37)… neón cian/turquesa (#2DD4BF) en la sección de coctelería… Playfair Display… Plus Jakarta Sans"; structure pinned by "Estructura y Secciones Obligatorias" 1–7.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
